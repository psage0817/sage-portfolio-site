hi, i’m sage!

i’m a second-year student at washu, majoring in communication design and minoring in psychology and human-computer interaction.

art has always been a huge part of my life, whether i’m singing on stage or painting in a studio. i’ve also always been curious about other people, which led me to journalism in high school. as i got older, those two interests came together in design, where i get to be creative and help people at the same time.

right now i’m most into ui/ux design, editorial design and social media graphics. when i’m not designing, i’m probably with my a cappella group (s.o. sens!), watching video essays, or trying a new cafe.
