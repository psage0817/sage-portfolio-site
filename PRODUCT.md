# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A mix of readers, none ranked above the others: design recruiters hiring for internships and junior UX/product roles, people who might hire Sage for web, brand or editorial work, and classmates, professors and programs. Most arrive from the home page or a case study and want to know who Sage is as a designer and as a person.

## Product Purpose
Sage Park's personal portfolio: selected work with case studies (invetty is complete), an About page and contact. Success is a visitor leaving with a clear sense of how Sage works and who they are, and getting in touch.

## Positioning
A human-centered designer based in St. Louis and San Diego whose site is built around their own room: the window from their home, their own photos of Del Mar and St. Louis, their handwriting font, their playlist. The site is personal by construction, not by decoration.

## Operating Context
Static site on GitHub Pages (plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step). Sage edits it in VS Code and pastes in files or merges pull requests.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: index.html (home), invetty.html (case study), about.html (not yet designed).
- Day and night follow the real local time of the city in the window.
- Undecided: Sage's school, major and year, and the a cappella group's name. Ask; never invent.

## Brand Commitments
- Lowercase voice ("hi, i'm sage park", "selected work").
- Type: Alte Haas Grotesk for headings and the wordmark, Satoshi for body text, Le Jardin Du Bonheur for handwriting (sticky notes).
- The room: bronze window frame, white sills and shelves, warm wall by day, lamp-lit wall at night.
- Avoid anything that reads as AI-made or overstimulating.

## Evidence on Hand
- invetty case study: led a team of four, 2026, FigJam and Figma.
- softspot cover; strike magazine stl, social media marketing and type collection are in progress.
- Sage's own photos of Del Mar and St. Louis (img/sky/).
- Art beyond UX: painting, photography, lettering and type.
- A cappella: singer, soloist, arranger/music director and in a leadership role in their group.
- Sage will supply real photos for the About page. None are in the repo yet.

## Product Principles
1. Personal, never generic: every detail should be true to Sage.
2. Calm over loud: one moment of delight per page, never constant motion.
3. The work and the person are equal: designer, artist, student and singer all belong.
4. Say plainly what isn't finished yet.
